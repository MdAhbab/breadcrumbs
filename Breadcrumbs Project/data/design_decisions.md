# Design Decisions & Architectural Rationale

This document details every design decision made during the implementation of the Breadcrumbs Synthetic Corpus Generator where the specification left choices open, along with the technical justification for each choice.

---

### 1. Wage & Grade Distribution Model (§1, §2.1)
* **Open Decision:** How to distribute wages across RMG grades (1 to 7) while maintaining stable site-level mean wages.
* **Choice:** We mapped Grade 7 (entry apprentice) to the base minimum wage of 12,500 BDT, with Grade 1 (senior master operator) receiving a ~1.65x multiplier. Individual basic wages are sampled from a Gaussian centered at `site_mean * grade_multiplier` with a coefficient of variation of 4%, clipped at the statutory minimum.
* **Defense:** Real garment factories operate on standardized grade pay scales; modeling grade variation prevents the synthetic data from looking like a single Gaussian cluster while preserving site-level mean wage stability.

---

### 2. Operational Physical Balance for Cross-Checking (§2.5, §7.6)
* **Open Decision:** The exact mathematical formulation connecting workers present, machine hours, production units, and electricity consumption in `production_output`.
* **Choice:** We implemented a physical pipeline:
  $$\text{Units} = \text{Workers Present} \times \text{Machine Hours} \times \text{Worker Efficiency} \times \text{Seasonality Boost}$$
  $$\text{Electricity (kWh)} = \text{Machine Hours} \times \text{Power Intensity (28-48 kWh/h)}$$
* **Defense:** In clean periods, aggregate labor hours reported in payroll correlate with total machine hours and production in `production_output`. This allows `cross_inconsistency` anomalies to perturb this ratio while leaving both documents individually and internally valid.

---

### 3. Check-Digit Implementations (§2.2, §2.3)
* **Open Decision:** Specific check-digit algorithms for certificates and chemical CAS numbers.
* **Choice:**
  - **ISO 45001 Certificates:** `ISO45001-NNNNNCC` where $CC = NNNNN \pmod{97}$. Corruption is generated by adding a non-zero offset $\pmod{97}$.
  - **CAS Numbers:** $R_n \dots R_2 R_1 - R_2 R_1 - C$, where $C = \left(\sum_{i=1}^k i \times R_i\right) \pmod{10}$.
* **Defense:** Standard modulo check-digit schemes reflect issuing body standards without requiring external network lookups.

---

### 4. Continuous Severity & Wave Drift Function (§3, §4)
* **Open Decision:** How continuous severity (0.0 to 1.0) and wave progress (0.0 to 1.0) combine to alter anomaly detectability.
* **Choice:** For arithmetic anomalies, the percentage discrepancy $\delta$ is:
  $$\delta = 0.015 + 0.35 \times \left(\text{severity} \times (1.0 - 0.5 \times \text{wave\_progress})\right)$$
* **Defense:** At the beginning of Wave 1, anomalies feature obvious 15-35% discrepancies. By the end of Wave 1, discrepancies drift down into the 1.5-3.5% range, providing a natural test of within-wave distribution shift.

---

### 5. Late Wave 3 Recurrence Mechanism (§4)
* **Open Decision:** When and how Wave 1 anomalies should recur in Wave 3.
* **Choice:** Wave 1 anomalies (`arithmetic`, `overtime`) are reintroduced during periods `2027-09` to `2027-12` (Q4 of Year 3) at a target recurrence rate of 1.5%.
* **Defense:** This tests whether continual learning models experience catastrophic forgetting on production streams without relying solely on held-out benchmarks.

---

### 6. Sharding Hierarchy & Format (§8)
* **Open Decision:** How to structure the shard filesystem hierarchy.
* **Choice:** Standard Hive-style partitioning:
  `documents/site=<site>/wave=<wave>/part-<period>-<site>.jsonl.gz`
* **Defense:** This partitioning enables downstream PyTorch and federated training pipelines to load specific site shards or specific wave partitions using standard filesystem globs.

---

### 7. Deterministic Gzip Header Fixing (§9)
* **Open Decision:** How to ensure `.jsonl.gz` output files produce identical SHA-256 hashes across different operating systems and execution times.
* **Choice:** We write compressed bytes via `gzip.GzipFile` with `mtime=0.0` and empty filename headers.
* **Defense:** Standard Python `gzip.open` embeds the current system wall-clock timestamp into the archive header, which breaks bitwise determinism across runs. Setting `mtime=0.0` guarantees byte-for-byte reproducibility.

---

### 8. Adversary Trace Resolution (§7)
* **Open Decision:** How to represent withheld and edited document versions in the corpus.
* **Choice:**
  - Withheld documents are fully generated, recorded in `ground_truth.json` with `is_withheld=True`, logged in `adversary_trace.json`, but separated during disclosed evaluation subsets.
  - Retroactive edits produce versioned documents (e.g. `doc_id-v2`) in the corpus, with the exact edit delta recorded in the trace.
* **Defense:** This allows scoring both the ledger's ability to prove completeness (detecting withholding) and immutability (detecting unauthorized revisions).
